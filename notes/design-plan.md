# Design plan: The Successor site

Subject: a carryable care robot (the lantern) for aging parents of autistic adults.
Audience: Stanford Longevity Design Challenge judges. Job: feel the problem, meet the robot, walk through real scenarios.

## Colour
- Dusk `#22304A`: storybook night sections (care happens in the evening)
- Night `#141C2F`: deepest backgrounds, text on light
- Lantern `#F3B33D`: the brand light, used only for the lantern's glow and primary actions
- Glow `#FFE7AE`: projected step text, highlights
- Mist `#E8EDF4`: the product panel and light sections (crisp, product-like)
- Mode colours: learning `#B9A7F0`, guiding `#F3B33D`, running a routine `#7FD1B9`, resting `#8A97B3`
- Safety light: runs alone `#3FB27F`, needs a person `#F08A3C`

## Type
- Literata (a typeface designed for long-form reading): headlines and story text. Book voice.
- Atkinson Hyperlegible (designed for low-vision readers): interface, labels, body in panels.
  Chosen because the subject is disability and legibility.

## Layout
- Hero: full-bleed dusk scene, a window with the lantern glowing on a table, headline left.
- Storybook sections on dusk; the robot viewer and safeguards on mist (product feel).
- Scenario explorer: list of scenarios left, a stage right. The stage draws the room and the lantern;
  the lantern's mode light, safety light and projected text change with every step.
- Story arc: six beats on an emotional curve (a real sequence, so numbering is used).
- A "See the lantern" drawer available anywhere; scenario steps open it on the feature in use.

## Principles
- The one bold thing: the lantern illustration that reacts to each step. Everything else is quiet.
- Left aligned. No eyebrows in caps, no arrows on buttons, no card grid with identical shadows.
- Motion only in response to the reader (stepping, opening the drawer), plus one hero glow.

## Review against defaults
First instinct was a cream background with a serif headline and terracotta accent: the generic look.
Changed to a dusk palette because evening is when the story's care happens and the lantern's light
reads best against it; product panels use cool mist rather than cream.
